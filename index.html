IN PROGRESS CASE STUDY / 001

PAYMENT ANALYTICS

UPI Payment Reliability, Failure Root-Cause & GMV Impact Analysis

End-to-end analytics project investigating payment reliability across one million synthetic UPI transactions to identify failure patterns, potential root causes and business impact.

Payment failures affect transaction completion and GMV. The analysis asks where failures occur, what drives them and which segments require attention.

Analysis spans failure rates by bank, app version, hour, failure reason and user behaviour, alongside transaction value and GMV-at-risk measures.

Early analysis identifies elevated failure rates for SBI and PNB, midnight hours and app version 4.9.0. Full findings will be published when the case study is complete.
